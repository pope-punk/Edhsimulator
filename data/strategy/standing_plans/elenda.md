**Mana and setup.** Treat Glasswing Grace as the 34th land when needed; keep a two-land opener only with Wayfarer’s Bauble or dependable acceleration. Establish both colors, then choose between accelerating to four mana and deploying an early payoff. Sol Ring, the Diamonds, Fellwar Stone, Orzhov Signet, Oasis Gardener, Solemn Simulacrum and Pristine Talisman support development; bank Lotus Blossom for a protected X-spell or double-spell turn rather than routine ramp.

**Lifegain architecture.** Put the payoff down before spending gain events. Ajani’s Pridemate, Elenda’s Hierophant, Essence Channeler, Fiendish Panda, Twinblade Paladin, Marauding Blight-Priest, Exemplar of Light and Dawn of Hope prefer many separate triggers; Defiant Bloodlord, Midnight Snack and transformed Sorin reward one large gain. Angel of Vitality and Leyline of Hope increase amounts but do not add events. Use extort, Pristine Talisman, Restless Fortress, Polluted Bonds and lifelink combat to generate distinct triggers; enable Exemplar on opponents’ turns where possible.

**Engines and resources.** Dawn of Hope turns gains into cards or lifelink bodies. Pontiff of Blight makes every other creature an extort source, so retain a cheap follow-up spell and value expendable tokens. Trading Post converts discard, artifacts and creatures across several axes: pitch Rune-Scarred Demon or Noxious Gearhulk before reanimation, cash Solemn for value, or sacrifice a token for a card. Tutors should find the missing function—mana, draw, protection, removal, evasion, reset or payoff—not automatically the largest finisher.

**Winning routes.** Track 41 life for Elenda’s 5/5 menace, 47 for Leyline’s team bonus, and 50 for Elenda’s 10/10 form. Arvad, Angelic Destiny, Celestial Armor, Swiftfoot Boots and Rogue’s Passage convert those thresholds into commander damage; Twinblade Paladin plus a power boost creates two lifelink damage steps. For burst drain, establish Sorin of House Markov before a large precombat gain, transform after combat, then use −1. Exsanguinate or Debt to the Deathless can feed Sorin, Bloodlord and Midnight Snack simultaneously.

**Interaction and politics.** Preserve efficient removal for engines, lethal attackers and graveyard threats; use Darksteel Mutation or Grasp of Fate when exile-like containment matters. High Priest of Penance combined with Valorous Stance or Celestial Armor can trade damage for nonland removal. Encourage opponents to attack and answer one another by accurately highlighting public threats, without exposing hidden information or making unsupported claims.

**Recovery and pivots.** Sequence recursion before Kunoros, since it shuts off Zombify, Restart Sequence, kicked Nullpriest, Fiendish Panda and Sorin recursion. Temporary power improves Hierophant’s death tokens and Panda’s reanimation ceiling; Essence Channeler transfers counters and Angelic Destiny returns. Before Akroma’s Vengeance, cash in replaceable artifacts and creatures; prefer a reset that leaves planeswalkers and a clear rebuild path. Reassess the primary route after a payoff is removed, a tutor resolves, graveyards become constrained, or a wipe changes resource posture.
