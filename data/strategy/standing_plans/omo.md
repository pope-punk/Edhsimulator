**1. Foundation and sequencing.** Mulligan for functional blue-green mana, not raw land count: two lands need Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Growth Spiral, Expedition Map, or a credible third-land route. Many lands enter tapped or produce colorless mana. Deploy Spelunking before tapped-land chains, and Dryad of the Ilysian Grove, Azusa, or Oracle of Mul Daya before spending extra land plays. Omo is usually the turn-three bridge from fixing into land-type engines.

**2. Everything-counter infrastructure.** Spread counters across durable, differently named lands. Each marked land supplies all basic types and all relevant nonbasic types, powering Cloudpost, Baldur’s Gate, Basilisk Gate, Desert Warfare, Sage of the Maze, and Urza lands while fixing colors. Planar Nexus naturally supports these packages and is Maze-searchable. Mark creatures only when Merfolk, Elf, or Illusion synergies justify it. With Omo and Aven Courier attacking, stack Courier below Omo so Courier can copy the new counter.

**3. Mana, value, and tutoring.** Convert typed lands into excess mana through Cloudpost/Trenchpost, the Urza trio, Magus of the Candelabra, Xolatoyac, Sage, and Mana Reflection. Use tutors to solve the active bottleneck—colors, a high-output land, interaction such as Talon Gates of Madara, or the missing half of a supported finish—rather than exposing a premature combo. Establish Tatyova and landfall engines before extra land drops; recycle lands with Simic Growth Chamber or Wonderscape Sage.

**4. Primary victories.** The compact line is Dark Depths copied after entry by Thespian’s Stage or Mirage Mirror, ideally at the last opposing end step; Vesuva and Copy Land enter with ice counters and do not shortcut it. Maze’s End requires ten differently named Gates and returns itself as an activation cost; copied names do not increase the count. Sunken Palace can copy valuable generic-cost activations or X spells, but its seven-card exile cost competes with graveyard plans.

**5. Wide and animated finishes.** Scute Swarm, Rampaging Baloths, Avenger of Zendikar, and Desert Warfare turn land velocity into boards. Sage can animate Gates; grow Jyoti with Basilisk Gate before combat to amplify the army. March from Velis Vel can make marked lands hasty copies of a large nonlegendary creature—avoid legendary copies. Helm of the Host on Omo multiplies counter triggers; Aggressive Biomancy and Replication Technique convert strong creatures or engines into decisive value.

**6. Interaction and recovery.** Preserve flexible removal and counters for effects that stop the chosen route. Whelming Wave can spare marked nonland creatures while Omo remains; Evacuation can return Omo to hand and avoid commander tax. Ramunap Excavator, Uro, and Desert Warfare recover sacrificed or milled resources; replay valuable graveyard lands and escape Uro before a large Finale of Revelation shuffles the graveyard. After a reset, rebuild colored mana and counter distribution first, then select whichever of Depths, Maze, tokens, or animated lands still needs the fewest exposed pieces.
