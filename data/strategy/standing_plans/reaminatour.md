**Development and commander.** Build dependable white, blue, and black mana with lands and the seven rocks; favor cheap smoothing or self-replacing permanents over hands clogged with expensive recursion targets. Aminatou, Veil Piercer is the deck’s indispensable discount and selection engine, not a combo piece to expose casually: normally deploy her by turn three or four when she immediately improves the line. Mystic Remora, Rhystic Study, Entity Tracker, Omen of the Sea, Baleful Strix, Spirited Companion, Mind Stone, and Mulldrifter supply the cards needed to keep developing.

**Miracle and library-top management.** Treat hand, graveyard, battlefield, and library top as one workspace. Sensei’s Divining Top, Brainstorm, Vampiric Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Hall of Heliod’s Generosity, and Aminatou the Fateshifter can stage an enchantment for the commander’s first-draw miracle; commander surveil can clear the card beneath it. Protect that first-draw window from optional Remora/Rhystic draws. Miracle discounts make costly enchantments efficient and can enable sorcery-speed enchantments during an opponent’s turn.

**Graveyard and access engines.** Stock the graveyard with surveil, Consider, Gifts Ungiven, Gravebreaker Lamia, Liliana, or Restoration of Eiganjo. Recover through Animate Dead, Dance of the Dead, Necromancy, Chthonian Nightmare, Karmic Guide, Reveillark, Vesperlark, Body Double, Sun Titan, Starfield of Nyx, Replenish, or Awakening Hall. Body Double is the flexible hinge; Gifts may deliberately find only two cards. Dimir House Guard both transmutes for key four-drops and can become a sacrifice outlet. Choose tutors and recursion pieces by the number of live connections they create.

**Combo grammar.** Repeated cores include Leonin Relic-Warder plus a reanimation Aura; Karmic Guide/Felidar Guardian sacrifice loops; Reveillark or Vesperlark with Body Double; Sun Titan plus Changing Loyalty; Aminatou the Fateshifter plus Felidar Guardian; and Starfield animating Parallax Wave or Touch the Spirit Realm. A loop must have a valid outlet and payload: entry/blink events are not death events. Viscera Seer, Fanatical Devotion, Fallen Ideal, or Dimir House Guard can bridge that gap.

**Conversion and control.** Grim Guardian and The Meathook Massacre are primary kills; Underworld Coinsmith, Funeral Room, Doomwake Giant, Entity Tracker, Victor, Liliana, and Ghostly Dancers convert enchantment entries, deaths, recursion, or repeated blinks into damage, control, cards, or the missing module. Chthonian Nightmare repeatedly produces energy and enchantment triggers while exchanging disposable value creatures. Distinguish a value/stabilization loop from a demonstrated kill, but recognize when deterministic draw, scry, or tutor access installs its own payoff.

**Protection, recovery, and posture.** Preserve Fierce Guardianship, Swan Song, Remand, Glen Elendra Archmage, Alseid, Fanatical Devotion, or Parallax Wave for the engine, payoff, or decisive stack window rather than incidental threats. Use Loran, Oblivion Ring, Touch, Changing Loyalty, Elspeth Conquers Death, Doomwake, and Meathook to survive. Prefer blockers and recursive value over chip attacks unless combat protects a planeswalker or completes a line. After disruption, rebuild through redundant recursion; after exile, pivot to another core. Reassess after tutors, mill, major draws, recursion exchanges, or board resets, identifying the best reachable core, missing role, acquisition path, timing constraint, and fallback.
