Minsc \& Boo, Timeless Heroes

Standing Plan — how to play this deck:

Use this document as durable deck doctrine, not as a turn script. At planning checkpoints, reread the supplied seed, inspect the current long- and short-term plans, and use `inspect deck` to search beyond cards already seen. Use `inspect roles` and focused role queries—especially `wincon`, `counter_synergy`, `engine`, `ramp`, `tutor`, `removal`, `combat_trick`, `protection`, and `card_advantage`—to recover the private strategic notes attached to relevant cards; inspect named cards and packages before committing to a line. The high-level job is to develop mana and counter multipliers, turn Boo or another concentrated threat into combat pressure, cards, and direct damage, and retain a protected conversion or rebuild route. Choose actions by asking what advances a supported win route, which multiplier or resource is missing, what interaction can punish concentration, and how the deck recovers from a reset. Let the short-term plan hold immediate combat and sequencing; revise the long-term plan only when the match-level route, resource posture, threat model, or recovery plan actually changes.

Keep the three planning layers distinct. This Standing Plan is immutable, reusable guidance for how to pilot the deck: its mechanics, win conditions, decision principles, inspection vocabulary, and recovery doctrine. The private Long-Term Plan is the determinate objective for this particular match: the current primary win route or development goal, posture toward every opponent, plausible future branches, and observable cues for pivoting. The private Short-Term Plan is the executable bridge between current reality and that goal: synthesize game status, inspections, and recent rationales into direct next actions, with explicit sequencing, priority and trigger timing, attacks, blocks, targets, interaction to hold, and hazards to avoid.

Deck Overview:

Mulligan for two or three lands, an untapped green source, acceleration, and access to red by the Minsc turn; Kazuul’s Fury may serve as a tapped red land, but hands led by several colorless utility lands are unreliable. The preferred sequence is a one-mana creature or Sol Ring, followed by Nature’s Lore or Three Visits for Stomping Ground, another accelerator, or a cheap counter enhancer, then Minsc on turn three—or turn two from Sol Ring and two colored lands. An enhancer is worthwhile before Minsc only if it does not delay him; after the Boo trigger resolves, another enhancer can still be cast before activating loyalty. Counter-replacement ordering matters enormously. Apply additive effects from Hardened Scales, Ozolith, the Shattered Spire, and Kami of Whispered Hopes before doublers such as Branching Evolution, The Earth Crystal, level-three Innkeeper’s Talent, and Vorinclex: a placement of N counters becomes (N + adders) × 2^doublers. Thus Minsc’s three counters become eight with one adder and one doubler, making a fresh Boo 9/9. Innkeeper and Vorinclex also multiply loyalty counters, while All Will Be One converts loyalty, +1/+1, proliferate, landfall, and Ozolith placements into damage. Terrasymbiosis draws the final number of counters placed and can trigger once on every player’s turn through Forgotten Ancient or Managorger Hydra. Bristly Bill and Evolution Sage turn every land into growth, Wooded Foothills produces two landfall events, and Kodama of the West Tree converts modified combat damage into still more landfall. Defiler of Vigor grows the entire board while discounting green permanents, whereas Fanatic of Rhonas and a grown Kami convert the first four-power creature into explosive mana.

Boo is a recyclable package of haste, damage, cards, and counters rather than a creature that must remain alive indefinitely. Ordinarily decline a redundant upkeep Boo, but with The Ozolith you can create the new token, put the old Boo into the graveyard through the legend rule, bank all its counters, and move them onto the hasty replacement at combat—where replacement effects may enlarge them again. This also removes disabling Auras or marked damage. Most turns favor Minsc’s +1 before attacking; once Boo is large enough, reserve the loyalty activation, attack, and use −2 postcombat so Xenagos, Unnatural Growth, Bulk Up, or Unleash Fury still increases both the damage and the Hamster draw. Taurean Mauler is also a Hamster because of changeling. Fling and Kazuul’s Fury provide instant-speed conversion and sacrifice as a casting cost, while Minsc sacrifices only as its ability resolves. Chandra’s Ignition can clear blockers before combat or exploit combat-phase power doubling afterward; Ram Through combines removal with trample damage. Wider finishes come from Kalonian Hydra, overloaded Fangs of Kalonia, Return of the Wildspeaker, Champion of Lambholt, and Bristly Bill, while Ghalta, Rogue’s Passage, and Kessig Wolf Run reward concentrating resources. Rishkar’s Expertise can cast a newly drawn five-mana finisher during its resolution. Heroic Intervention protects every permanent, while Inspiring Call protects only countered creatures but also refills the hand; either can make Blasphemous Act asymmetric. Delighted Halfling can make Minsc uncounterable, but Rhythm of the Wild and Domri protect creature spells rather than the planeswalker. After a reset, rebuild from Minsc’s next Boo, an Ozolith transfer, Eternal Witness, Fanatic’s eternalize, or Bulk Up’s flashback instead of redeploying every engine at once.



Standing Plan Summary (reusable heuristics):

- Develop untapped green mana and acceleration first, secure red on schedule, and cast Minsc early unless doing so would expose the deck to a clearly avoidable tempo loss.
- Sequence additive counter effects before doublers, and decide before combat whether Boo is being preserved, used as pressure, or converted into cards and damage after its power is maximized.
- Keep protection or an instant-speed conversion available when the table can answer the concentrated threat; avoid deploying every counter engine into the same reset.
- Use landfall, Ozolith transfers, and the next Boo as recovery routes. Tutor or draw toward the missing role rather than merely the largest creature.
- Reassess after each major counter multiplier, protection exchange, board reset, or opponent threat. Record the intended combat/conversion line and interaction threshold as dynamic updates.
- Aggressively use table talk to instigate conflict among opponents: emphasize public threats, encourage opponents to spend attacks and interaction on one another, and exploit those exchanges without revealing hidden information or presenting unsupported claims as fact.
